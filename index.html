02 / THE ARTIST

Manya Mehrotra.

StudioM is a dialogue between emotion, form and space.

Manya creates contemporary wall art through colour, texture and intuitive composition. Each piece is made to carry its own visual identity while becoming part of the room around it.

The practice moves between bold figurative work, abstraction, texture and colour-led pieces for homes, hospitality spaces and private commissions.
